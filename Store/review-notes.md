# App Review notes

Pasted into the "Notes" field of the App Review Information section by
`python Store/listing.py review-details`. Everything below the divider is the note.

---

No account or login is required.

HOW TO USE: tap CHOOSE PHOTO or CHOOSE VIDEO and pick any item. The picture is rebuilt out of text characters that have weight and move. Use the three Style buttons (Fluid, Burst, Drift) and the two sliders to change the effect, then tap EXPORT to render a video and open the share sheet.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. One optional non-consumable, "Glyphstorm Pro" (com.mattbusel.asciimotion.pro), one-time, no subscription. Everything can be made and exported for free; free exports carry a small GLYPHSTORM mark in the bottom right corner, are rendered at screen resolution, and videos stop at 5 seconds. Pro removes the mark, renders at twice the resolution and runs videos to 15 seconds. To see the paywall: tap GLYPHSTORM PRO at the bottom of the home screen, or open any photo and tap GO PRO under the EXPORT button. RESTORE PURCHASE is on the paywall.

SCREEN RECORDING (Guideline 2.1): https://mattbusel.github.io/glyphstorm/review.mp4 shows the app launched from the home screen, a photo chosen through the system picker, the three styles, both sliders, an export with the share sheet, and the Pro paywall.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE: a creative effect app. It turns the user's own photos and videos into animated text-character artwork to post or keep. For adults and teens who make short visual content; rated 4+.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: none. No network requests, no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no AI service and no third-party SDKs. Rendering is Metal on the device. Purchases go through StoreKit only.

PERMISSIONS: none. The app uses the system photo picker, so it receives only the single item chosen and never asks for photo library access. Export goes through the system share sheet.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: none. Identical in every region.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL: not applicable. All art, text and code are my own work. Store screenshots use a generated image, not a photograph.
