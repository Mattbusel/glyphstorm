GLYPHSTORM

Pictures, rebuilt out of characters that move.

SUPPORT

Something wrong, or an idea? Email mattbusel@gmail.com and say which iPhone or iPad you are on.

HOW IT WORKS

Choose a photo or a video. Pick Fluid, Burst or Drift. Set how much movement and how big the characters are. Export, and share it anywhere.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Where do my exports go? Wherever you send them. Export opens the standard share sheet, so you can save to Photos, send to a friend, or drop it in any app.

Why is my long video shorter after export? Exports are capped at 15 seconds. Past that the render takes long enough that it looks like the app has hung.

Does it need my photo library? No. It uses the system picker, so it only ever sees the one file you choose, and it never asks for library access.

Is anything uploaded? No. There is no network code in the app at all.
